# v14 changes
- Staff/admin sidebar: full-height, brand on top, profile card, grouped icons, Settings at the bottom; search is in the top bar. Code: `menuGroups`, `NAV_ICONS` in `public/layout.js`; styles at the end of `public/layout.css` (section "STAFF AND ADMIN SHELL"). To revert the look, delete that CSS section.
- Dashboard now opens inside the same shell: `public/dashboard.html` shows `/dashboard/?embed=1` (React build in `dashboard/`, source in `dashboard-src/`).
- Handler menu: My Sessions, Session orders, Dashboard, Cat schedule, Staff status, FAQ.
- New Staff status screen (`staff-status.html`): who is working, on break, lunch, on leave or off. Leave form moved here from My Sessions.
- Pop-ups (`uiAlert/uiConfirm/uiPrompt`) replace the browser's grey boxes.
- Guests have a username (sign up + Settings) and can log in with it. `node set-password.js <username or email> "<password>"`.
- Sessions conclude by themselves when the time is over; staff can conclude early or press No-show (room and cats are freed).
