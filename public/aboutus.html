About Us

Welcome to Purrsona Meow! 🐾

Welcome to Purrsona Meow, a cosy little haven created for cat lovers and their furry companions! Whether you are looking to unwind, enjoy some quality time with cats, or simply take a break from your busy day, our café offers a warm and relaxing environment where everyone can feel at home.

Our Story 🐱

Purrsona Meow was created out of a genuine love for cats and a desire to give them a happier, safer and more comfortable environment. We believe that every cat deserves love, care and a place where they can feel safe. We also understand that not everyone has the opportunity to keep a cat at home, even if they love them. That is why we wanted to create a space where people can enjoy the company of cats, make memories and experience the happiness that these little companions bring.

Our Mission ❤️

Our mission goes beyond providing a place for people to enjoy their time with cats. We hope to contribute to the well-being of cats, especially those that have been abandoned or are in need of care. We want to create awareness about responsible cat ownership and encourage kindness towards animals. Every cat deserves a chance to live a safe and happy life.

A Place for Every Cat Lover 🐈

Whether you are a lifelong cat lover or simply looking for a little furry companionship, Purrsona Meow welcomes you! Spend time with our lovely resident cats, enjoy their playful personalities and experience the happiness they bring. Have a cat of your own? You are welcome to bring your furry companion along too! Our café provides opportunities for cats to socialise, play and make new friends in a supervised environment. We understand that not every cat feels comfortable around unfamiliar cats. That is why we also offer private rooms where owners and their cats can enjoy some quiet time together in their own space.

A Little Space of Their Own 🏡

Every cat has its own personality. Some love making new friends, while others prefer a quieter environment. Our private rooms provide a comfortable alternative for cats that need their own space or are not ready to interact with other cats. We want every visitor, both human and feline, to feel safe, relaxed and comfortable during their visit.

Found a Cat in Need? 🐾

Sometimes, a little kindness can make a big difference. If you come across an abandoned, stray or vulnerable cat that needs help, please reach out to us. We would love to hear from you and see how we can help provide the care and support that the cat needs. Together, we can make a difference, one little paw at a time!
