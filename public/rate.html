Rates

We charge per hour, not per guest. The price is the same however many guests come (up to the session's limit). You pay after you book.

Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel any time before the session starts. Your room, cats and handler are then free for other guests.

If the café has to cancel (for example a cat is unwell or a room has a problem), you get a full refund.
